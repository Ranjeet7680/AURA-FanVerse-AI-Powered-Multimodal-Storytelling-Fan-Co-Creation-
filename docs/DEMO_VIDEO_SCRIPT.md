# AURA FanVerse: 3-Minute Demo Video Script
**Target Duration:** 3 minutes (180 seconds)  
**Format:** Split screen (Presenter + Live Web Prototype Screen Capture)

---

### [0:00 - 0:30] The Hook: The Visibility & Engagement Crisis
* **Visual:** Montage of packed women's stadiums juxtaposed with empty/sparse vertical social feeds. Presenter on screen.
* **Speaker:**
  > "Women's sports are experiencing an explosive global boom. Yet, when you look at social feeds and live interactive engagement, less than 12% of digital broadcast clipping goes to women's tournaments. 
  > Younger fans don't want to just sit through a 3-hour broadcast—they want instant vertical stories, tactical understanding, and direct connection with the athletes.
  > Welcome to **AURA FanVerse**, the AI-powered storytelling and tactical companion built to redefine fan engagement in women's sports."

---

### [0:30 - 1:15] Feature 1: Autonomous Micro-Narrative Engine
* **Visual:** Switch to live screen recording of AURA FanVerse web application.
* **Actions on Screen:**
  - Click on the **Micro-Narratives** tab.
  - Show a live match moment (e.g., Shafali Verma's six or Renuka Singh's inswinging wicket).
  - Toggle languages: Click **"Hindi"**, then **"Spanish"**, then **"Tamil"**—demonstrating real-time translated captions and synthesized excitement commentary.
* **Speaker:**
  > "AURA directly ingests live broadcast feeds and ICC ball-by-ball telemetry. In under 45 seconds, our multimodal AI identifies the turning points, crops the footage to vertical 9:16 reels, overlays dynamic stats, and generates authentic commentary across 12 regional languages. 
  > What used to take a 10-person production crew hours now happens automatically at the edge."

---

### [1:15 - 2:05] Feature 2: Interactive Tactical Co-Pilot
* **Visual:** Navigate to the **Tactical Co-Pilot** screen.
* **Actions on Screen:**
  - Click on the prompt: *"Why did Harmanpreet set a deep backward square leg in the 14th over?"*
  - The AI responds within seconds with tactical reasoning, match context, and shot probability.
  - The **Interactive 2D Field Visualizer** highlights the fielder's position on the oval, bowler line, and batter hot zone.
* **Speaker:**
  > "Fans want to get inside the captain's mind. With our Tactical Co-Pilot, fans can ask natural language questions during live play. 
  > Powered by multimodal RAG over live telemetry, AURA explains field changes, shows dynamic 2D field coordinates, and calculates live win-probability impact. It transforms passive viewing into a masterclass in cricket tactics."

---

### [2:05 - 2:35] Feature 3: Athlete Spotlight & Micro-Sponsorship Hub
* **Visual:** Navigate to the **Athlete Hub** tab.
* **Actions on Screen:**
  - Highlight emerging grassroots players and international stars.
  - Click "Send Micro-Support (50 Fan Tokens)" to unlock an exclusive badge and support the athlete's training fund.
* **Speaker:**
  > "AURA directly empowers athletes. Fans can unlock digital collectible badges and send micro-sponsorships. 
  > 15% of all platform micro-transactions flow directly into our ICC Future Stars Fund—sponsoring kits and travel for emerging female athletes across associate nations."

---

### [2:35 - 3:00] Conclusion & Vision for Dubai AI Festival 2026
* **Visual:** Presenter on screen with Dubai AI Festival / ICC Hackathon slide backdrop.
* **Speaker:**
  > "AURA FanVerse isn't just an idea—it's a pilot-ready, cross-sport platform that makes women's sports more visible, interactive, and sustainable. 
  > We are thrilled to take this journey forward to the Dubai AI Festival in 2026 and elevate the game for athletes and fans worldwide. Thank you!"
