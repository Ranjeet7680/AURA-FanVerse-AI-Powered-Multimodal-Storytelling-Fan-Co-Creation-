## SLIDE 2: The Macro Landscape & The Paradox
### Explosive Growth Meets Digital Invisibility

* **The Boom:** 
  * Global women’s sports viewership has grown over **300%** since 2020.
  * Stadium sellouts at Melbourne, Lord’s, and Mumbai illustrate massive physical demand.
* **The Structural Inequity:**
  * **Only 12% of digital sports social clipping** and broadcast syndication covers women’s fixtures compared to men’s.
  * Emerging associate women’s teams (e.g., UAE, Scotland, Thailand, Netherlands) receive virtually zero automated highlight coverage.
* **The Economic Bottleneck:**
  * Outside the top 1% of international superstars, emerging women cricketers lack digital commercial footprints, making grassroots sponsorships nearly impossible.

---

## SLIDE 3: The Gen-Z & Modern Fan Behavior
### Why Legacy Sports Broadcasting Is Failing the Next Generation

* **The Attention Shift:**
  * Fans under 25 consume **78% of sports content via vertical short-form video (9:16)** on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
  * Average attention span for single-match linear broadcast viewing has fallen below 22 consecutive minutes.
* **Demand for Participatory Fandom:**
  * Younger audiences don't just want to watch; they want to interrogate decisions: *"Why was that fielder placed there?"*, *"What was the swing speed?"*, *"How does this over impact win probability?"*
* **The Language Barrier:**
  * Over 65% of international cricket fans prefer commentary in regional/native dialects (Hindi, Tamil, Arabic, Spanish, Bengali), but live broadcasts offer at most 2–3 languages due to production overhead.

---

## SLIDE 4: The Core Problem Statement
### The Three Inefficiencies Breaking Sports Engagement

1. **The Broadcast Clipping Bottleneck:** Manual production teams take 2–6 hours to edit, format, subtitle, and distribute highlight packages, missing the real-time viral window.
2. **The "Passive Viewing" Deadlock:** Linear commentary is broad and generic, providing no interactive way for fans to explore tactical strategy or learn the sport's nuances.
3. **The Grassroots Funding Divide:** No direct, transparent micro-sponsorship vehicle connects global digital fans directly with female grassroots talent.
